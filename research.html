Topic 01

Tidal Wetland Dynamics

I develop dense satellite time-series models, including DECODE, to monitor tidal wetland extent, condition, and disturbance across large regions. A key challenge is to separate real wetland change from tidal-stage and inundation effects in the satellite record.

The work supports continuous monitoring in conterminous US tidal wetlands and Canadian tidal marshes, from long-term extent change to condition decline and disturbance hotspots.

Topic 02

Disturbance and Resilience

I use dense satellite time series to study disturbance and recovery as one process: when damage happens, how fast ecosystems recover, and whether another event arrives before recovery is complete.

We applied this idea to Florida mangroves, where repeated hurricanes can shrink the recovery window and push damaged forests toward ghost forest or open-water transitions. I also use temporal autocorrelation (AC1/TAC1) as a separate indicator of resilience, especially for detecting weakening recovery capacity.

Topic 03

Dense Time Series for Land Surface Monitoring

Dense satellite time series make some hidden land-surface features visible because timing can carry information that a single image cannot. Instead of depending on one clear image, this work uses seasonality and short-lived phenological windows.

Understory vegetation is a good example. It is usually hidden by the forest canopy, but an early-spring window before canopy closure lets Sentinel-2 time series detect understory plant communities.

Topic 04

Water Dynamics

I developed image-processing methods for automated surface-water mapping. This work handles common errors in optical water detection, especially confusion with built-up surfaces and terrain or cloud shadows.

With Sentinel-2 and Landsat observations, these studies improve water-index design, reduce urban background noise, and map monthly surface-water extent at 10 m resolution.

Selected papers

  • Yang et al., 2018, Remote Sensing of Environment
  • Yang et al., 2020, Remote Sensing of Environment

Topic 05

VHR Image and 3D Modeling

My earlier work used VHR oblique aerial imagery for urban image matching and building extraction, and photogrammetry for built-heritage documentation. It connects geometric image interpretation with practical reconstruction workflows.
